Tilmoch — Maxfiylik siyosati

Oxirgi yangilanish: 2026-yil 3-noyabr

Tilmoch — o'zbek tilidan rus, ingliz va arab tillariga (va aksincha) internetsiz ovozli tarjima qiluvchi ilova. Ushbu sahifa ilova qanday ma'lumotlardan foydalanishini tushuntiradi.

Qisqacha

  • Nutqni tanish va tarjima to'liq telefonning o'zida bajariladi. Ovozingiz va matnlaringiz hech qayerga yuborilmaydi.
  • Ilovada ro'yxatdan o'tish, reklama, kuzatuv yoki analitika yo'q.
  • Biz hech qanday shaxsiy ma'lumot to'plamaymiz va uchinchi shaxslarga bermaymiz.

Mikrofon

Mikrofon faqat siz tinglash tugmasini bosganingizda yoqiladi. Yozilgan ovoz faqat telefon xotirasida (operativ xotirada) qayta ishlanadi, matnga aylantirilgach o'chiriladi va hech qayerga saqlanmaydi yoki yuborilmaydi.

Internet

Internet faqat til paketlarini (nutqni tanish va tarjima modellarini) yuklab olish uchun ishlatiladi. Paketlar GitHub (github.com) va Hugging Face (huggingface.co) serverlaridan yuklanadi. Yuklash vaqtida bu serverlar, har qanday veb-sayt kabi, qurilmangizning IP manzilini ko'radi — ular o'z maxfiylik siyosatlariga amal qiladi. Paketlar yuklangach, ilova internetsiz ishlaydi.

Telefonda saqlanadigan ma'lumotlar

Tarjimalar tarixi, sevimli iboralar va sozlamalar faqat telefoningizda saqlanadi. Ularni ilova ichida o'chirishingiz yoki ilovani o'chirib yuborish orqali butunlay yo'q qilishingiz mumkin.

Bildirishnomalar

Bildirishnomalar faqat til paketlarini yuklab olish jarayonini ko'rsatish uchun ishlatiladi.

Ovoz chiqarib o'qish

Tarjimani ovoz chiqarib o'qish uchun telefoningizdagi tizim ovoz dasturidan (masalan, Google nutq sintezi) foydalaniladi. U telefoningiz sozlamalari va o'sha dastur siyosatiga muvofiq ishlaydi.

Bolalar

Ilova hech kimdan, jumladan bolalardan ham, shaxsiy ma'lumot to'plamaydi.

O'zgarishlar va aloqa

Siyosat o'zgarsa, ushbu sahifa yangilanadi. Savollar bo'lsa: github.com/yahyogulamov-max/tilmoch-models/issues

Tilmoch — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Tilmoch is an offline voice translator between Uzbek and Russian, English and Arabic. This page explains how the app handles data.

Summary

  • Speech recognition and translation run entirely on your device. Your voice and text are never sent anywhere.
  • The app has no accounts, ads, tracking or analytics.
  • We do not collect any personal data and do not share any data with third parties.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you press the listen button. Audio is processed in memory on the device, discarded after it is converted to text, and is never stored or transmitted.

Internet

Internet access is used only to download language packs (speech recognition and translation models) from GitHub (github.com) and Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Like any website, these servers can see your device's IP address while downloading and follow their own privacy policies. Once the packs are downloaded, the app works offline.

Data stored on your device

Translation history, saved phrases and settings are stored only on your device. You can delete them in the app or by uninstalling it.

Notifications

Notifications are used only to show language pack download progress.

Text-to-speech

Reading translations aloud uses your device's system text-to-speech engine (for example, Google Speech Services), which operates under your device settings and that engine's policy.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. Questions: github.com/yahyogulamov-max/tilmoch-models/issues
